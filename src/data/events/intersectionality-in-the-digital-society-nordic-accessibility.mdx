## Description

Digital accessibility work often focuses on Single-axis Analysis—considering people as “only” disabled while assuming they otherwise lead “normal” lives, with digital competencies, a supportive family, sufficient income, etc.

We will explore the concept of intersectionality: what it means, why it matters, and how we can begin incorporating it into our work. 😊
