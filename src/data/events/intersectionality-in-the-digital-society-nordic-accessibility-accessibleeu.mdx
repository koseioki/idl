## Description of the presentation

Work on digital accessibility often starts from a single-axis analysis, where we assume that people “only” have one disability.

At the same time, we risk overlooking how other factors, such as financial circumstances, digital skills, or access to support, affect an individual’s opportunities to participate in the digital society.

We will explore the concept of intersectionality: What does it mean, why is it important, and how can we begin to integrate it into our work?

The programme will be conducted in Danish. 

## Speaker

Kosei is a researcher and consultant at Intersectional Design Lab, with a background in applied informatics, UX, and intersectional studies.

He has been involved with several NGOs focusing on intercultural communication, disability rights, queer communities, and related areas.
